# Metrics

## Success metrics

| # | Metric | What it means | Before (December) | Target (May) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Final list on time | Final list locked at least 3 weeks before exams | Hall tickets 7 days before exams | 21+ days before |
| 2 | Ineligible students in the exam hall | Students who wrote the exam but weren't allowed to | 212 | 0 |
| 3 | Students without a seat | Students who turned up with no seat or paper | 38 | 0 |
| 4 | Question papers printed correctly | Gap between papers printed and students who actually wrote | Up to 30% wasted or short | Within 3% |
| 5 | Payments settled before exams | Payments matched to students, and refunds resolved, by exam day | 40 shown unpaid; 25 refunds unresolved | 100% matched, 0 unresolved |

## Guardrail metrics

These check that the fix doesn't create new problems.

| # | Guardrail | What it means | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eligible students registered | Students who were allowed to write and completed registration | 98% or higher |
| 2 | Students wrongly blocked | Students blocked who later turn out to be eligible | Under 0.5% |
| 3 | Unfair late fees | Students charged a late fee because a rule changed | 0 |
| 4 | Changes after lock | Hall tickets amended after publishing | 1% or fewer of students, none in the last 2 days before exams |
| 5 | Exam-status help-desk tickets | Students asking "where do I stand?" | Half of December's volume |

How these are measured: almost all of them come from data the module already records: lock date, registrations, payments, refunds, amendments and notices. Exam-day seat issues and help-desk tickets come from the exam cell and the help desk.

The targets (3%, 98%, 0.5%) are proposed starting points, to be agreed with the CoE.
